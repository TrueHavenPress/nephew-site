Privacy policy

Plain version: we only collect what you send us, we use it to do the work you asked about, and we don't sell it to anybody.

What we collect

The only information we collect is what you type into the contact form or tell us on the phone:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • The town you're in
  • Whatever you write in the "What's going on?" box
  • Any photos you choose to attach—these are always optional

We don't run advertising trackers on this site, and we don't ask you to accept cookies, because we don't set any.

About the photos

Photos are optional. If you send them, we use them for one thing: to understand the job before we come out, so we arrive prepared with the right tools and materials.

We will not publish a photo of your home, post it on social media, or use it in any advertising unless you tell us in writing that we can. If we'd like to use a photo of finished work, we'll ask you first, and "no" is a perfectly fine answer.

How long we keep things

We keep inquiry messages and any attached photos for up to two years, so we can look back at what you originally asked for if you call us again. Records tied to work we actually performed—estimates, invoices, and job notes—are kept for seven years, because tax and business rules require it.

If you'd like your inquiry and photos deleted sooner, just ask. Call 828-230-5329 or email jjs.handymansolutions@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.

Who else sees it

We don't sell your information, rent it, or trade it. We don't share it with marketing companies.

A small number of ordinary business services necessarily handle it along the way—our email provider, our phone carrier, and the service that delivers this website's contact form to our inbox. They process it on our behalf so we can read your message. We may also share information where the law requires it.

Fonts on this site

The type on this website is served by Google Fonts. When your browser loads a page here, it requests those font files from Google, and that request includes your IP address. We don't receive that information and we don't use it for anything, but we'd rather tell you than not.

Children

This site is meant for homeowners and property owners. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Reach us at 828-230-5329 or jjs.handymansolutions@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change how any of this works, we'll update this page. If it's a significant change, we'll say so here rather than quietly editing it.

Questions
